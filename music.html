About Yellow Taxi

The Hindustan Ambassador isn't just an automobile, it is an absolute icon of Indian automotive history. Originally based on the British Morris Oxford Series III and manufactured in Uttarpara, West Bengal, the "King of Indian Roads" served as the default chariot for India's prime ministers, politicians, and military officers for decades.

But its true soul belongs to the streets of Calcutta. Painted in a vivid, unmissable yellow to catch the eye even in the dead of night, the yellow taxi became the emotional heartbeat of the city. For over a century, these taxis have stood at the crossroads of tradition and modernity.

More than just a mode of transport, the yellow taxi became a silent, non-living character deeply woven into Bengali culture. Through literature, paintings, and the cinematic masterpieces of legendary filmmakers like Satyajit Ray and Mrinal Sen, the taxi has spent a century narrating the untold stories of a culturally rich metropolis.

About the Playlist & Art

This page is a tribute to that same sense of journey and nostalgia. It features a curated Bengali music playlist that leans heavily on classic melodies, Anupam Roy hits, Arijit Singh staples, and golden-era masterpieces. Just like a long cab ride through the city at night, the music is meant to evoke deep emotion and memory.

The Playlist is created by Shreyasee and the background artwork was generated using DALL-E, deeply inspired by the beautiful visual aesthetics of The Taxi Project by Subhabrata Bose.
